# Sync new mail only

The MVP organizes only mail received after a connected account is authorized rather than importing historical mailbox content or pre-accepting existing contacts. This keeps onboarding faster, reduces privacy exposure and sync cost, and avoids applying the Screener retroactively to old conversations. The trade-off is that the app may feel sparse immediately after onboarding and familiar people will still appear in the Screener the first time they email after setup.

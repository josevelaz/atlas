# Use app-owned email organization

The MVP treats categories, read state, archive state, trash/delete state, handling states, AI priority, summaries, and extracted action items as app-owned metadata rather than syncing them back to Gmail or Outlook. Provider-backed actions are limited to sending replies and new messages. This avoids surprising users by changing read status, reorganizing, archiving, or deleting mail in their existing mailbox while still allowing Atlas to provide its own opinionated organization layer.

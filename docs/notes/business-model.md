# Business Model

The working business model is freemium with paid individual subscription upgrades.

The free tier should include one connected account and limited AI usage so users can experience the AI assistant before paying. Paid tiers may unlock multiple connected accounts, higher AI usage limits, more semantic search capacity, advanced automation, and future integrations.

B2B/company sales may be explored later, but the MVP is not built around workspace, admin, or seat-based billing concepts.

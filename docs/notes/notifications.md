# Notifications

## MVP

The MVP uses PWA notifications only for important Inbox activity, especially high-priority threads. Feed and Paper Trail do not notify by default. Unscreened senders do not notify by default unless the AI identifies the screened thread as potentially high priority or urgent.

This supports the product promise of protected attention: notifications should surface what likely needs the user's attention, not recreate inbox noise.

## Future

Future versions may allow configurable notification rules per category, connected account, sender, or priority level.

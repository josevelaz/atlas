# Audience

The product targets a mixed audience:

1. Teams and companies with high-volume email workflows.
2. Newsletter-heavy consumers who want cleaner separation between important mail, subscriptions, and paper-trail messages.
3. Busy professionals and founders who need prioritization, summaries, and action extraction.

The MVP is personal-first and uses individual accounts only: each user manages their own connected accounts, Screener, categories, handling states, and AI assistant. There is no workspace, organization, admin, seat billing, shared inbox, or team-collaboration concept in the MVP. The product may later be sold into companies as a smarter individual inbox for employees, but shared-inbox and team-collaboration workflows are deferred.

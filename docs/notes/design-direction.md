# Design Direction

The app should use a brutalist / neobrutalist visual direction rather than a conventional polished SaaS aesthetic. The interface should still feel fast, usable, and native-like as a PWA.

Motion and interaction design should support tactile brutalist motion: bold, direct, tactile, and responsive rather than soft, floaty, bouncy, or overly decorative. Buttons should feel pressable through shadow/translate changes, panels should move quickly and geometrically, and route/category transitions should feel native-like without slowing down email triage.
